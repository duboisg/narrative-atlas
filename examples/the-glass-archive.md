# The Glass Archive

Mara Venn arrived at the floodwall archive before sunrise. The building's glass stacks held
the city's legal memory, but a storm had cut its cooling system and the oldest records were
beginning to delaminate. Ilya, the night custodian, refused to unlock the restricted vault
without a magistrate's order. Mara showed him the rising water line instead. Together they
opened the vault and began moving boxes upstairs while the emergency lights faded.

## The Missing Ledger

At noon, Mara discovered that the harbor ledger was absent from its numbered shelf. Ilya
admitted that Councillor Saye had requested it the previous evening. The ledger contained the
original floodwall easements, evidence that several private towers had been built across public
spillways. Mara understood why the current flood was climbing so quickly. She asked Ilya to
help her recover the ledger before the council sealed the district.

## A Choice at the Gate

They found Saye's courier at the north gate, trying to leave with a waterproof case. Ilya
blocked the vehicle while Mara transmitted photographs of the ledger to the city newsroom.
The courier abandoned the case as water crossed the road. Mara could preserve the archive or
help evacuate the lower district, but not both. She left the records to the pumps and joined
Ilya at the rescue boats.
